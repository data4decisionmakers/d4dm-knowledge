 ## Summary

 The language of Communicating Sequential Processes (CSP) is a
powerful tool for understanding concurrent behaviour.  It models
parallel execution and inter-process communication, it supports a
practical theory of correctness and refinement, and it is attractive
and intuitive to use in specification.
