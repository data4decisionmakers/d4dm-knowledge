 ## Summary

 Structured data refers to organising information according to well-defined constraints.
While historically this largely meant using relational databases, the growth of markup languages, document databases and other 'NoSQL' approaches has led to a range of approaches for specifying more flexible structures.
This course covers the two leading technologies for data interchange in this space: XML and JSON.
It will benefit those wishing to gain an in-depth understanding of the relative merits of these technologies, and best practices for their use.
