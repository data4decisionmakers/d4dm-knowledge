 ## Summary

 To govern information requires mastering a diverse, portfolio of legal and ethical rules, technology standards, business policies, and technologies, all applied across increasingly complex, distributed systems and repositories. Businesses and public agencies are under increased scrutiny regarding information governance and are being required to demonstrate compliance to standards and openness regarding their uses of data.
